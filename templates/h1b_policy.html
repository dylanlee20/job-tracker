H1B Visa Policy Information

Comprehensive guide to H-1B visa requirements, process, and policies for finance professionals

Last Updated: February 2026 | Information is subject to change. Always verify with official sources.

Overview

The H-1B visa is a non-immigrant visa that allows U.S. employers to temporarily employ foreign workers in specialty occupations. Investment banking, finance, quantitative analysis, and technology roles commonly qualify for H-1B sponsorship.

Key Points
  • Annual Cap: 85,000 total visas (65,000 regular cap + 20,000 advanced degree exemption)
  • Duration: Initial period of 3 years, extendable to 6 years total
  • Lottery System: When applications exceed cap, lottery determines selection
  • Filing Period: Registration typically opens in March each year
  • Start Date: October 1st is the earliest employment start date

Application Timeline

March (Early)

Registration period opens - Employer registers electronically with USCIS

March (Late) - April

Lottery conducted - Selected registrations receive notification

April - June

Selected applicants file complete H-1B petitions with supporting documentation

October 1

Earliest start date for new H-1B employment (fiscal year begins)

Recent Policy Changes (2024-2026)

  • 2024: Electronic registration system became mandatory for all H-1B cap-subject petitions
  • 2025: Increased scrutiny on specialty occupation requirements and beneficiary qualifications
  • 2026: New wage level requirements implemented to ensure prevailing wage compliance
  • Ongoing: Site visit protocols remain in effect for worksite verification
